Hi! I'm Vaibhav, I study (primarily) statistics as a sophomore at the National University of Singapore!

Other than statistical theory or applied math in general, some of my career interests lie in the multidisciplinary approach of statistical methodology in many fields of research. It is imperative to understand the standing of a formal education in statistics as is necessitated in these fields -- it goes far beyond data literacy (that NUS covers gracefully for every single student in [GEA1000](https://nusmods.com/courses/GEA1000/quantitative-reasoning-with-data)) and being able to read numbers. 

I love the thrill of fearlessly attempting out things outside my comfort zone. Aside an expository research stint I did a few years ago in simple differential geometry and the theory of curves and invariants, I also have been able to translate my passion in teaching by dint of the School of Computing here, taking up the commitment of being a teaching assistant for the course [CS1010S](https://nusmods.com/courses/CS1010S/programming-methodology), a programming course meant to introduce students to a formal treatment of programming methods and abstraction-oriented problem solving.
