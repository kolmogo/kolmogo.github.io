About me

Hi! I'm Vaibhav, a sophomore at the National University of Singapore, primarily studying statistics.

Other than statistical theory or applied math in general, some of my career interests lie in the multidisciplinary approach of statistical methodology in many fields of research. It is imperative to understand the standing of a formal education in statistics as is necessitated in these fields -- it goes far beyond data literacy (that NUS covers gracefully for every single student in GEA1000) and being able to read numbers.

I love the thrill of fearlessly attempting out things outside my comfort zone. Aside from an expository research internship stint I did a few years ago in simple differential geometry and the theory of curves and invariants (which I will upload a long post about here, later), I've also had the opportunity to pursue my interest in teaching as a teaching assistant for CS1010S (Programming Methodology) at the NUS School of Computing. The course introduces students to programming methods, abstraction, and computational problem-solving.

  • Probability and stochastic processes
